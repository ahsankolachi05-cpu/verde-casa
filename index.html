About Us

Care, Rooted in Murcia.

Verde Casa exists for one simple reason: a home in Murcia deserves looking after by people who know and love the region. Whether you live here year-round or visit when you can, we take care of the practical side — so your home is always as welcoming as the day you arrived.

Every arrangement starts with a conversation, not a contract. We listen to what your home needs, agree a plan that fits, and keep you informed in the language you're most comfortable with.

  • One point of contact for every service
  • Plans tailored to each individual property
  • Friendly updates after every visit
